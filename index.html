How CampusFix Works

A transparent 3-step pipeline connecting student reports to facility technicians

01

Snap & Submit

Describe the issue, pinpoint the building and room, and optionally attach a photo. Your report generates a trackable Ticket ID.

02

Review & Dispatch

Maintenance leads inspect urgency, allocate technicians (HVAC, plumbing, electrical), and publish real-time progress notes.

03

Verified & Resolved

Once repairs are inspected, the ticket is marked resolved. Students can confirm and close the loop with zero paperwork.
